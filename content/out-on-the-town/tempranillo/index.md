If you’re looking for authentic Spanish cuisine in the Aspen area, Tempranillos does not disappoint. It has a lovely outdoor seating area too. 

Highly recommend the Roasted Brussels Sprouts - incredibly crispy! They also came with a very flavorful aioli sauce.

The Tostada Iberica was great too. Jámon Iberico was paired well with bread and tomatoes to create a tasty dish.

Tempranillos paella was authentic and flavorful as well. Their paella emphasized seafood - both versions included clams, mussels, and squid. The Valencia version added chorizo and chicken to add some additional flavor.
