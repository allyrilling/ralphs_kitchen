If you’re looking for a tapas / paella experience in the Aspen area, Tempranillos does not disappoint. It has a lovely outdoor seating area too. 

Highly recommend the Roasted Brussels Sprouts - incredibly crispy! They also came with a very flavorful aioli sauce.

The Tostada Iberica was great too. 

Tempranillos paella was authentic and flavorful.
