Wingra Boats Full Moon Paddle is a must for any Madison local. The staff set up canoes with fire pits in the middle of the lake for paddlers to take their vessels too for a tasty treat.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgdmgrse0/image/upload/c_crop,g_north_west,h_1481,w_1296,y_411/v1788661968/IMG_8717_vrk0mb.jpg)

They all set up a buffet so you can fuel up before paddling or afterward if you didn't get enough to eat. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgdmgrse0/image/upload/v1788661968/IMG_8689_js2sb3.jpg)

Ralph recommends bringing your own items for roasting if you make it out. Ralph was very jealous of those who did and will be sure to add his own flavor to the festivities next time. - Love Ralph
