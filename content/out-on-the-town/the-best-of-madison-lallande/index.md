Lallande is Ralph’s favorite restaurant in Madison. The food is fresh and the flavors are vibrant. Their menu changes with the season so be sure to check it out different parts of each year. Lallande is owned by the creators of Bloom Bake Shop and they bring their pasty prowess to their dishes. This spring featured a fish baked in puff pastry.
