Lallande is Ralph’s favorite restaurant in Madison. The food is fresh and the flavors are vibrant. The cover photo is a farm to table beet salad. Their menu changes with the season so be sure to check it out different parts of each year. Lallande is owned by the creators of Bloom Bake Shop and they bring their pasty prowess to their dishes. This spring featured a fish baked in puff pastry. Here are some photos of that dish and others from our visit this spring:

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgdmgrse0/image/upload/v1786547696/IMG_0523_kzpuem.jpg)

An appetizer that included salmon on savory french toast. One of the favorites of the night, surprising flavors. Ralph nearly jumped out of his boots.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgdmgrse0/image/upload/v1786547699/IMG_0520_hppoux.jpg)

Duck. Spinach. Orange Marmalade. Simple and delicious, showing french flavors can have a wide range. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgdmgrse0/image/upload/v1786547709/IMG_0522_lxhuvb.jpg)

Desert number 1, why not have more than one desert. This one was buttermilk ice cream, chocolate cake and candied pecans. A unique combinations of flavors, and a close second to the final desert.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgdmgrse0/image/upload/v1786547680/IMG_0524_hyvxwi.jpg)

Desert number two a Basque Gateau. This was out of this world. It tastes like a lemon sable cookie with a smoother texture. One of Ralph's favorite deserts, highly recommended.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgdmgrse0/image/upload/v1786547703/IMG_0525_lpgefp.jpg)
