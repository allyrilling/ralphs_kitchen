Ralph was lucky to go to Pasture and Plenty for valentine's day last year. We had a 5 course meal, which was one of our favorite valentine's day dinners. The highlights were the kolarobi, goat cheese, and fig salad along with the braised short rib. The short rib came with a celery puree and a door county cherry glaze.



![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgdmgrse0/image/upload/v1786709132/IMG_0144_labhrb.jpg)

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgdmgrse0/image/upload/v1786709110/IMG_0146_xpjmci.jpg)



The chocolatey desert at the end of the meal also came with a cherry component and was an excellent way to end date night. Be sure to get on Pasture and Plenty's email list if you want to make one of their special dinners. The dinner is prix fixe and you need to pay online via their website first.
