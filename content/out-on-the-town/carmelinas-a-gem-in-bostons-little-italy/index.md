Ralph made a pit stop in little Italy before flying out of Logan this spring. The appetizers and pasta were amazing. The Tuna Arrabbiata is pictured in the cover photo and was probably the best dish although it's hard to pick a favorite. Complementry garlic bread was also provided. It was soft and the olive oil and balsamic paired with it was quite nice.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgdmgrse0/image/upload/v1778959290/IMG_7917_agzv0i.jpg)

Main courses included the Crazy alfredo and the Rollati. Take a guess at which is wish. The Rollati is a well kept secret. Amongst the other guests we were the only one's daring enough to order it and other folks wanted to know what we had so give it a try if you're lucky enough to get a reservation. You'll definitely need one, this is one of the most popular establishments in the area. - Love Ralph
