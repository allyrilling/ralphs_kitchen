Ralph had a fantastic French experience. He tried his best to order the whole menu but was almost asked to leave early after ordering only 3 appetizers. Said appetizers - mussels, raclette, and a savory summer crepe (more crepes to come) - were quite good. 

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgdmgrse0/image/upload/v1786839819/IMG_8525_mqae31.jpg)

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgdmgrse0/image/upload/v1786839804/IMG_1244_qcgyjg.jpg)

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgdmgrse0/image/upload/v1786839816/IMG_1243_jhu5yu.jpg)

The main courses were a chicken fricasse and a lamb cannoli. The chicken was the best Ralph has had, so crispy yet tender. The cannoli was great too and a first time for Ralph.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgdmgrse0/image/upload/v1786839809/IMG_8527_uknse4.jpg)

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgdmgrse0/image/upload/v1786839799/IMG_1245_kohuxx.jpg)

Obviously desert was most important. Ralph had two crepes. A crème brûlée crepe and a pistachio crepe - both were easily the best Ralph has ever had. The crème brûlée crepe was innovative and delicious. I still think of it every evening before bed. - Love Ralph
