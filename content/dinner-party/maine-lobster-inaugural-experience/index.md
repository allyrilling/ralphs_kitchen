First time eating whole boiled lobster. An interesting experience… don't recommend drinking from the Lobster's head but Mainers may argue otherwise. Regardless, a must if you find yourself in the state on your travels. Stop by a local shop and ask them to boil your lobster for you, then take it home and have at it. Try your best not to get Lobster guts on your hosts. That's a good way to find trouble. We were quite careful... - Love Ralph
