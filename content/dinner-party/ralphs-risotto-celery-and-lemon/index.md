Ralph has a risotto problem. He's tried many recipes at this point in his culinary adventures and this one was a highlight. Ralph decided to make [this risotto recipe ](https://www.jamesbeard.org/recipes/celery-root-and-lemon-risotto) from the Jame's Beard Foundation. The risotto was garnished with fresh celery leaves and lemon confit. Ralph made celery stock for the risotto from scratch and made a celery puree for the risotto to rest on. A variety of vibrant flavors showed that celery can be much more than just a peanut butter snack and should be the secret ingredient in your next fancy meal.

![](https://res.cloudinary.com/dgdmgrse0/image/upload/v1788660132/IMG_1954_cum3g8.jpg)

Salmon baked with lemon and paper was the main protein for the meal along with a small side of sheet pan veggies. Ralph loves fresh produce and now is a great time of year to find some at your local grocer or farmers market. - Love Ralph
